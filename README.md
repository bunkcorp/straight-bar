# Straight Bar

A Mac practice desk for 6-string lap steel in C6 (C E G A C E, low to high).

The lessons cover the bar, tuning, straight-bar sixths, blocking, vibrato, slants, an intonation ladder, and turning a sixth into a ninth. Later lessons walk the sixths up the neck (C6 through Bb6 and back to C6), the minor triads on strings 3–2–1, one forward slant and one reverse slant, a B diminished triad, right-hand rolls, a chord-tone line past fret 7, and an original I–IV–V form. After that: palm blocking on the high strings, thirds and fourths beside the sixths, a C6 inversion with the root off string 6, a four-bar melody with a sixth under each note, natural harmonics, a G9–C6 vamp and a short intro, whole-note backup with I IV V on the screen, slides into a note, and an eight-bar take-off. The practice desk and tuner stay available the whole time.

## Songs

Open **Songs** in the header. The catalog is built in: Aloha ʻOe (chorus), Near the Cross, the original Trade Wind etude, and I–IV–V in C. Song melodies are public domain, in the key of C, with original fret charts for this tuning. Search stays on the machine. Play scrolls the chart into a now-line, lights the fret, and plays a backing band. Guide can be turned off. Listen scores a note when the microphone is within 25 cents. With Listen off, the chart still runs and you follow the lights.

## Sixths fretboard

Open **Sixths** in the header. It walks the eight C-major sixths (thumb on the lower note, middle finger on the higher one), names a straight-bar sixth at any fret, and can hide the answer so you can find a scale note yourself. Play scale sounds all eight pairs.

## Theory

Open **Theory** in the header. The course walks the staff, rhythm, scales, intervals, chords, and progressions in original words for this C6 neck, with a picture on each screen and something to hear or tap.

## Launch

Double-click `Start Straight Bar.command`, or from this folder:

```bash
npm install
npm start
```

The first launch installs Electron. macOS will ask for the microphone when you press Listen. Allow Electron under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone. Headphones keep the tuner from hearing the speakers.
